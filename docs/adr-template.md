## Context

What forces the decision: the problem, the facts that constrain it, and what is expensive to undo. Link
the issue and the documents it touches instead of copying them.

## Decision

What was decided, in the present tense and in enough detail that someone can tell later whether the code
follows it. Number the parts when there are several.

## Options considered

### A. The chosen option

Why it fits.

### B. Another option

What it gives, and why it was not chosen.

## Trade-offs

What the chosen option costs: what gets harder, what is given up, and what would make the decision worth
revisiting.

## Consequences

What changes now: the files, workflows, contracts and documents that follow from the decision, the
versions it affects (standard, section 7), and what a reader must do next.
